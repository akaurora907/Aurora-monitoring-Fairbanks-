Understanding the readings

Bz: Sustained negative values are generally favorable. Around -5 nT is useful; -10 nT or lower can be especially favorable.

By: Helps describe magnetic-field orientation. Its effect depends on other solar-wind and magnetospheric conditions.

Bt: Total magnetic-field strength. Stronger fields can be more influential, especially when Bz is southward.

Solar wind speed: Faster solar wind can increase energy transfer into Earth's magnetosphere.

Density: Describes the number of solar-wind protons. Higher density can increase dynamic pressure, but is not automatically better.

Kp: A global three-hour index. It is not a direct measure of visibility in Fairbanks.

OVATION: A model forecast, not a guarantee of visible aurora at your location.
