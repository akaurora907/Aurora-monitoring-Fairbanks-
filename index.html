Understanding the readings

Bz: Sustained negative values are generally favorable. Around -5 nT is useful; -10 nT or lower can be especially favorable.

Solar wind speed: Below 350 km/s is slow; 350–500 is moderate; 500–600 is fast; above 600 is very fast.

Density: Below 5 is low; 5–10 is moderate; 10–20 is elevated; above 20 particles/cm³ is high.

Kp: A global three-hour index. It is not a direct measure of visibility in Fairbanks.

OVATION: A model forecast, not a guarantee of visible aurora at your location.
